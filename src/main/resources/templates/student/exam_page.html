Before you begin

Please review the following rules before starting your exam.

  1. Time limit: The timer starts after you click Start Exam.
  2. Automatic submission: The exam is submitted automatically when the timer expires.
  3. Answer selection: Select an option to record an answer. Unanswered questions may receive zero marks.
  4. Mark for review: You can mark questions to revisit them before submitting. Marking for review does not select an answer.
  5. Answer changes: You may change answers before final submission.
  6. Fair conduct: Do not use unauthorized assistance or share answers.
  7. Final submission: You cannot change answers through this attempt after submission.
  8. Technical issues: Contact your exam administrator if a technical problem occurs.
Important: Avoid refreshing or closing the exam page. Your progress may not be recoverable unless it is saved by the server.
